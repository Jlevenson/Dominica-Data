Carapace length

Spread of curved carapace length (notch to tip) of measured encounters: one violin per species, or one per season when a species is picked. White dot = median, dark bar = middle half of values. Groups with fewer than 5 measurements are shown as dots. Lengths outside 120–190 cm for leatherbacks, and 20–200 cm for other species, are treated as entry errors and left out.

About the data
  • Built from 18 DomSeTCO source tables (the WIDECAST/DomSeTCO database export 2003–2014, the full ArcGIS Survey123 layer exported 9 Sep 2026, yearly masterdocs and sighting sheets, the 2023+ backup sheet and field forms scanned in Nov 2026), merged and de-duplicated into one row per encounter. Back-entered ArcGIS records for seasons up to 2021 were 4 h behind the source records and were corrected.
  • "Not recorded" means the source left the species, beach or outcome blank or unreadable.
  • Withheld from this public version: flipper tag numbers, observer names and GPS positions (nest locations are available on request). Patrol shifts logged with no turtle (mostly 2020–2021) are left out.
  • Tagged turtles: within each species, tags recorded together on at least two nights are one turtle; a single shared night can add a new tag to a turtle but never merges two known turtles. Leatherback records under 120 cm CCL are left out of this.
  • Patrol effort was not logged in most seasons, so counts reflect both nesting and how much beach was watched.

Data: DomSeTCO beach patrol records, compiled by Oceans Forward. Download the tables behind this page: encounters.csv, turtle_seasons.csv. See the README for columns and how to cite.
